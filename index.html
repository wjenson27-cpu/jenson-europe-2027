Sat–Mon May 29–31, 2027 · London

Westminster & St James

Hotel booked

Westminster and St James put you in walking distance of Big Ben, Westminster Abbey, St James’s Park, and Buckingham Palace—classic London power-corridor vibes with parks and river views rather than nightlife. The Conrad sits across from St James’s Park Underground, so day trips (Tower Bridge, museums, West End) are easy without fighting traffic. Early-summer evenings are long; a Thames-side walk at blue hour is the simplest “wow” moment.

  • Afternoon tea near St James / Mayfair if you want the ritual

Sat Jun 5, 2027 · Docked 10:30 am – 8:30 pm

Messina · Etna & Taormina

Hold $795

Messina is the Strait’s gateway—useful as a launchpad rather than a full-day town. Classic shore day: cliff-top Taormina (Greek theatre, Corso Umberto, Etna views) and/or Mount Etna lava landscapes. Expect a scenic but time-boxed day; the harbor itself is rebuilt/modern after earthquakes, with the Duomo and astronomical clock as the in-town highlight if you stay local.

Shore plan

  • Private Etna/Taormina · ItalyTours.EU · HOLD $795 (email hold — not marked fully paid on sheet)
  • Celebrity timing: docked 10:30 am – 8:30 pm · typically alongside (not tender)
  • Taormina ~1–1.5 hr each way depending on traffic
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Messina cruise terminal · Taormina theatre

Must-try

  • Arancini — Sicily’s iconic fried rice cones
  • Cannoli with fresh ricotta (eat same-day, not packaged)
  • Granita + brioche if it’s hot after Etna
  • Swordfish / seafood if you stay harbor-side
  • Pasta alla Norma (eggplant + ricotta salata)

Don't miss

  • Pick Etna OR Taormina depth — both full is rushed
  • Messina Duomo astronomical clock if you stay in town
  • Cash/card for roadside gelato stops
  • Return buffer — dock until 8:30 pm but traffic bites

Mon Jun 7, 2027 · Docked 8:00 am – 5:00 pm

Katakolon · Ancient Olympia

Hold $820

Tiny Ionian harbor town that exists largely for cruise guests—cafés on the waterfront, then a short inland transfer to Ancient Olympia, birthplace of the Olympic Games. The stadium starting blocks, Temple of Hera, and museum sculptures are the payoff; the vibe is dusty stone, olive hills, and “standing where athletes stood in 776 BC.”

Shore plan

  • Private Olympia · Niki Olympic Tours · HOLD $820
  • Celebrity: docked 8:00 am – 5:00 pm · village walkable; Olympia ~25–40 min by road
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Katakolo · Ancient Olympia

Must-try

  • Greek salad with real feta + olive oil
  • Souvlaki / gyros back in Katakolon harbor
  • Fresh grilled octopus if the taverna looks busy with locals
  • Loukoumades (honey doughnuts) for a sweet finish
  • Iced freddo espresso in the heat

Don't miss

  • Ancient Olympia stadium starting line + Temple of Hera
  • Archaeological Museum if legs allow
  • Hat, water, decent shoes — dusty site, June sun
  • Harbor stroll after the site — easy wind-down before all-aboard
Tue Jun 8, 2027 · Docked 8:00 am – 6:00 pm

Corfu · Paleokastritsa

Candidate $685

Corfu mixes Venetian fortresses and pastel lanes in UNESCO Old Town with postcard coves at Paleokastritsa (cliffs, monasteries, glass-clear water). The island feels greener and more “Ionian garden” than Cycladic whitewash. Best day split: morning Paleokastritsa boat/beach, afternoon Liston / Spianada / Old Fortress.

Shore plan

  • Paleokastritsa-style shore · Viator candidate · ~$685 — booking status unclear on sheet (TBD)
  • Celebrity: docked 8:00 am – 6:00 pm at New Port · Paleokastritsa ~30–45 min by road
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Corfu New Port · Paleokastritsa

Must-try

  • Pastitsada — Corfu’s spiced meat + pasta classic
  • Sofrito (garlic-wine veal) if on a traditional menu
  • Kumquat liqueur tasting (island signature)
  • Fresh seafood by the Liston or Paleokastritsa cove
  • Baklava / galaktoboureko with Greek coffee

Don't miss

  • Split day: Paleokastritsa water + Old Town fortresses
  • Walk the Liston / Spianada — Venetian Corfu at its best
  • Old Fortress views if heat allows
  • Swim kit for Paleokastritsa — water is the point
Wed Jun 9, 2027 · Docked 10:00 am – 7:00 pm

Split · Blue Lagoon & Trogir

Candidate $300+

Split is one of the Adriatic’s easiest walk-off ports: palm-lined Riva leads straight into living Diocletian’s Palace—Roman cellars, peristyle, and cafés inside a 4th-century retirement fortress. For a boat day, operators run Blue Lagoon swims plus UNESCO Trogir’s compact marble old town. Vibe: Roman stone + modern promenade energy.

Shore plan

  • Splitilicious Blue Lagoon / Trogir · Viator candidate · ~$300–415 (sheet ~$300) — status TBD
  • Also DIY Diocletian’s Palace on foot from the pier (~5–10 min)
  • Celebrity: docked 10:00 am – 7:00 pm · usually alongside near the Riva
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Diocletian’s Palace · Blue Lagoon

Must-try

  • Gregada / fish stew or simply grilled catch of the day
  • Peka (slow-baked meat/octopus under the bell) if available
  • Black risotto (crni rižot) — Adriatic classic
  • Pašticada with gnocchi (Dalmatian Sunday energy)
  • Rakija sip + green olives on the Riva

Don't miss

  • Walk Diocletian’s Palace — cellars + peristyle
  • Decide: palace depth or Blue Lagoon / Trogir boat — both is tight
  • Gelato on the Riva before all-aboard
  • Easy walk-off port — no need to rush a shuttle for Old Town
Thu Jun 10, 2027 · Docked 8:00 am – 6:00 pm

Bar → Historic Kotor

Booked · Celebrity

Bar is Montenegro’s main commercial seaport—sunny waterfront and olive country—but the scenic jackpot is the fjord-like Bay of Kotor. Historic Kotor’s walled lanes, cathedral square, and fortress stairs deliver the Venetian Adriatic postcard. Expect a coach day (~1.5–2+ hours each way depending on traffic).

Shore plan

  • BOOKED — Celebrity Historic Kotor excursion
  • Celebrity: docked at Bar 8:00 am – 6:00 pm · industrial port; shuttle common to town “zero point”
  • Kotor is an excursion — not a walk from the pier
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Port of Bar · Kotor Old Town

Must-try

  • Njeguški pršut (smoke-cured ham) + local cheese
  • Seafood buzara in Kotor if you lunch in town
  • Kajmak with warm bread
  • Montenegrin olive oil tasting near Bar if you stay local
  • Local wine (Vranac) by the glass

Don't miss

  • Historic Kotor walls / cathedral square — the day’s jackpot
  • Fortress stairs only if knees + heat cooperate
  • Expect a long coach day — snacks + motion plan
  • Bar port is industrial — use the shuttle; don’t wander with a tight clock
Fri Jun 11, 2027 · Docked 8:00 am – 6:00 pm

Dubrovnik · Walls & Cable Car

DIY

Dubrovnik’s limestone Old Town and circuit of city walls are the Adriatic’s headline act—terracotta roofs, cliff drops to the sea, and Stradun’s marble parade. The Srđ cable car buys the wide bay panorama when legs are done with stairs. Go early or late if possible; mid-day wall walks in June heat + ship crowds are intense.

Shore plan

  • DIY: city walls, cable car, Game of Thrones photo spots — no paid tour confirmation on sheet
  • Celebrity: docked 8:00 am – 6:00 pm at Gruž (~3 km from Old Town) — bus/taxi/shuttle to Pile Gate
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Gruž port · Pile Gate · Cable car

Must-try

  • Fresh oysters / mussels from nearby beds if on menus
  • Black risotto or grilled squid off Stradun side streets
  • Rozata (Dubrovnik caramel custard)
  • Avoid the most touristy plazas for dinner — one alley over is better
  • Cold draft beer after the wall walk

Don't miss

  • City walls early — June midday heat is real
  • Srđ cable car for the bay panorama
  • Bus/taxi from Gruž to Pile Gate (not a casual walk)
  • Cash for wall tickets; water + hat mandatory

Sun Jun 13, 2027 · Docked 7:00 am – 6:00 pm

Naples · Pompeii / Sorrento / Amalfi

Hold $1,025

Naples is loud, delicious, and Vesuvius-framed—pizza homeland and gateway to Pompeii’s frozen Roman streets. A classic coast day stacks ruins with Sorrento cliffs and the hairpin drama of Amalfi / Positano; it’s a long, scenic grind, so prioritize 2–3 stops max. Build generous return buffer before all-aboard.

Shore plan

  • Private Pompeii / Sorrento / Amalfi · ItalyTours.EU · HOLD $1,025
  • Celebrity: docked 7:00 am – 6:00 pm at Stazione Marittima · Amalfi roads are slow
OpenStreetMap · Google Maps · Naples port · Pompeii · Positano

Must-try

  • Pizza Napoletana — Margherita or Marinara from a classic oven
  • Sfogliatella + espresso for breakfast fuel
  • Fried pizza (pizza fritta) as a street snack
  • Amalfi: limoncello / lemon granita if you reach the coast
  • Ragù napoletano if you stay in the city for lunch

Don't miss

  • Prioritize 2–3 stops max (Pompeii + Sorrento OR Amalfi — not everything)
  • Pompeii: water, shade, good shoes; skip midday if heat is brutal
  • Amalfi roads are slow — build a fat return buffer
  • All-aboard discipline — last port night before 5 am arrival
